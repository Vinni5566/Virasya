# Design Philosophy: Rustic Digitalism

Virasya's design is inspired by the organic textures and earthy tones of Indian handicraft traditions. It aims to feel "hand-made" yet high-performance.

## 🎨 Color Palette (HSL)

- **Primary (Terracotta)**: `19 37% 46%` - Represents clay, earth, and traditional firing.
- **Background (Handmade Paper)**: `30 15% 95%` - A warm, off-white beige that reduces eye strain.
- **Accent (Deep Maroon)**: `344 60% 50%` - Used for verification badges and highlights.
- **Secondary (Sand)**: `19 20% 90%` - Soft backgrounds for cards and inputs.

## 🖋 Typography

- **Headlines**: `Alegreya` (Serif) - Evokes a sense of literary heritage and tradition.
- **Body**: `PT Sans` (Sans-Serif) - Ensures high readability for product descriptions and navigation.

## 🧱 Visual Elements

- **Texture**: A subtle "Handmade Paper" overlay is applied globally via `globals.css`.
- **Radii**: Large border radii (`3xl`, `[40px]`, `[60px]`) are used to mimic the soft, rounded edges of pottery and hand-woven textiles.
- **Depth**: Soft shadows (`shadow-sm`, `shadow-xl`) provide a layered feel without looking "techy."

## 📱 Responsiveness

The UI follows a mobile-first approach, recognizing that many Indian artisans interact primarily through mobile devices. The navigation and upload flows are optimized for touch and simplified gestures.
