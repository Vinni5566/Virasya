# **App Name**: Virasya

## Core Features:

- Artisan Product Creation & Management: Allows artisans to upload product images, provide basic details, and manage their product listings stored in Firestore.
- AI Product Listing Content Tool: Generates SEO-friendly product titles, detailed descriptions, tags, and authentic craft stories using an AI tool based on artisan inputs and product images.
- AI Artisan Marketing Assistant: Provides smart price guidance and generates compelling social media marketing content (captions, hashtags) for artisan products using an AI tool.
- Marketplace Discovery & Filtering: Enables buyers to browse products in a responsive grid layout, apply filters (e.g., craft type, region, price, artisan), and search, with product data fetched from Firestore.
- Product Detail & Cultural Story Pages: Presents comprehensive product views, high-quality images, descriptions, artisan profiles, and the AI-generated cultural craft story, retrieved from Firestore.
- AI-Personalized Product Recommendations: Offers buyers tailored product suggestions based on their browsing history and preferences, leveraging an AI tool and Firestore data.
- Secure Role-Based Authentication & Profiles: Implements Firebase Authentication to manage distinct artisan and buyer roles and user profiles stored in Firestore, securing access to relevant features.

## Style Guidelines:

- The primary color is a rich, earthy terracotta (#A1664A), evoking the warmth of handmade crafts and heritage.
- A soft, warm off-white (#F4F2F0) serves as the background, reminiscent of natural paper or cream-colored fabric.
- The accent color is a muted berry/rose (#CC335C), providing a vibrant yet harmonious contrast for interactive elements and highlights.
- Headlines use 'Alegreya', a humanist serif font, to convey an elegant, intellectual, and contemporary sense of heritage.
- Body text utilizes 'PT Sans', a humanist sans-serif font, for its modern yet warm readability across various screen sizes.
- Minimalistic, elegant outline icons or subtly stylized line-art icons that complement the rustic and handmade aesthetic.
- Utilize rounded cards and soft, subtle shadows for product listings and content blocks to create a warm, inviting, and handcrafted feel.
- Fully responsive design for optimal viewing and interaction across mobile, tablet, and desktop devices.
- Subtle and elegant animations for transitions and interactive elements, avoiding overly flashy effects to maintain a calm and refined user experience.
